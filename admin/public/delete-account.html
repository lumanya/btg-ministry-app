BTG Ministry app

Delete your account

You can delete your BTG Ministry account at any time. Deleting it permanently removes:

  • your name, email address and password
  • your reading history, streak, and saved sermons and devotionals
  • prayer requests you sent while signed in
  • your likes and views

This can’t be undone. Nothing is kept afterwards, except copies in encrypted backups, which are overwritten within 7 days.

In the app

  1. Open the BTG Ministry app and sign in.
  2. Tap the person icon at the top of Home to open Your account.
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete my account, then confirm.

Without the app

If you no longer have the app, contact the ministry (details at btgministry.org) from the email address on your account, asking for your BTG Ministry app account to be deleted. We will delete it within 7 days and reply to confirm.

Programu ya BTG Ministry

Futa akaunti yako

Unaweza kufuta akaunti yako ya BTG Ministry wakati wowote. Kuifuta huondoa kabisa:

  • jina lako, barua pepe na nenosiri
  • historia yako ya usomaji, mfululizo, na mahubiri na tafakari ulizohifadhi
  • maombi uliyotuma ukiwa umeingia
  • ulivyopenda na kutazama

Hili haliwezi kutenduliwa. Hakuna kinachobaki, isipokuwa nakala kwenye hifadhi fiche za akiba, ambazo hufutwa ndani ya siku 7.

Kwenye programu

  1. Fungua programu ya BTG Ministry uingie kwenye akaunti.
  2. Gusa alama ya mtu juu ya Nyumbani kufungua Akaunti yako.
  3. Shuka chini ugusie Futa akaunti yangu, kisha uthibitishe.

Bila programu

Kama huna programu tena, wasiliana na huduma (maelezo yako btgministry.org) kutoka barua pepe ya akaunti yako, ukiomba akaunti yako ya programu ya BTG Ministry ifutwe. Tutaifuta ndani ya siku 7 na kukujibu kuthibitisha.
