BTG Ministry app

Privacy policy

Effective 25 November 2026

The BTG Ministry app is published on Google Play by Churchy Codes (Dar es Salaam, Tanzania) for BTG Ministry (Building the Temple of God), which provides its content. In this policy “we” means Churchy Codes and BTG Ministry together. This page explains what the app collects, why, and how you can remove it. We do not sell your information, show ads, or track you across other apps and websites.

What we collect

  • If you create an account: your name and email address, so you can sign in on any phone. Your password is stored only in encrypted (hashed) form.
  • Your reading and library: devotionals you have read, your reading streak, and sermons and devotionals you saved. If you are signed in this is kept with your account; otherwise it stays on your phone.
  • Prayer requests: the message you write and, only if you choose to add them, your name and a way to contact you. Only the ministry’s prayer team can read them.
  • Notifications: if you turn them on, a notification address for your phone, the kinds of notifications you chose, and your language, so we can send them.
  • Likes and views: when you like or open a sermon or devotional, we count it. If you are not signed in, it is counted under a random number made on your phone that does not identify you.

The app does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos, and it has no advertising or analytics trackers.

Services we use

  • Supabase stores accounts and the information above.
  • YouTube plays sermons and livestreams. When you play a video, YouTube’s own privacy policy applies.
  • Spotify (Spotify for Creators) hosts the podcast; episodes play from its servers. Instagram hosts the short “Seeds” videos and event posters. Like any website, these services receive your phone’s internet address when the app loads audio, video or pictures from them.
  • Expo, Apple and Google deliver notifications to your phone.

Deleting your information

In the app, open Settings → Delete my account (tap the person icon at the top of Home). This permanently deletes your account, reading history, saved items and the prayer requests linked to it. You can also ask us to delete it. Information kept only on your phone is removed when you uninstall the app.

Children

The app is for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. For questions, email info@churchycodes.co.tz or see the support page.

Programu ya BTG Ministry

Sera ya faragha

Inaanza kutumika 25 Novemba 2026

Programu ya BTG Ministry imechapishwa kwenye Google Play na Churchy Codes (Dar es Salaam, Tanzania) kwa ajili ya BTG Ministry (Building the Temple of God), inayotoa maudhui yake. Katika sera hii “sisi” ni Churchy Codes na BTG Ministry kwa pamoja. Ukurasa huu unaeleza programu inakusanya nini, kwa nini, na jinsi unavyoweza kuiondoa. Hatuuzi taarifa zako, hatuonyeshi matangazo, na hatukufuatilii kwenye programu au tovuti nyingine.

Tunachokusanya

  • Ukifungua akaunti: jina na barua pepe yako, ili uweze kuingia kwenye simu yoyote. Nenosiri lako huhifadhiwa kwa njia fiche pekee.
  • Usomaji na maktaba yako: tafakari ulizosoma, mfululizo wako wa kusoma, na mahubiri na tafakari ulizohifadhi. Ukiwa umeingia huhifadhiwa kwenye akaunti yako; vinginevyo hubaki kwenye simu yako.
  • Maombi ya kuombewa: ujumbe unaoandika na, ukipenda tu, jina lako na njia ya kuwasiliana nawe. Timu ya maombi ya huduma pekee ndiyo inaweza kuyasoma.
  • Arifa: ukiziwasha, anwani ya arifa ya simu yako, aina za arifa ulizochagua, na lugha yako, ili tuweze kuzituma.
  • Kupenda na kutazama: unapopenda au kufungua hubiri au tafakari, tunahesabu. Usipoingia kwenye akaunti, huhesabiwa kwa namba ya nasibu iliyotengenezwa kwenye simu yako isiyokutambulisha.

Programu haitumii mahali ulipo, anwani zako, kamera, kipaza sauti wala picha zako, na haina matangazo wala vifuatiliaji.

Huduma tunazotumia

  • Supabase huhifadhi akaunti na taarifa zilizo hapo juu.
  • YouTube hucheza mahubiri na matangazo mubashara. Unapocheza video, sera ya faragha ya YouTube hutumika.
  • Spotify (Spotify for Creators) huhifadhi podikasti; vipindi huchezwa kutoka kwenye seva zake. Instagram huhifadhi video fupi za “Mbegu” na mabango ya matukio. Kama tovuti yoyote, huduma hizi hupokea anwani ya mtandao ya simu yako programu inapopakua sauti, video au picha kutoka kwao.
  • Expo, Apple na Google hufikisha arifa kwenye simu yako.

Kufuta taarifa zako

Kwenye programu, fungua Mipangilio → Futa akaunti yangu (gusa alama ya mtu juu ya Nyumbani). Hii hufuta kabisa akaunti yako, historia ya usomaji, vitu ulivyohifadhi na maombi yaliyounganishwa nayo. Unaweza pia kutuomba tuifute. Taarifa zilizo kwenye simu yako pekee huondolewa unapoondoa programu.

Watoto

Programu ni kwa watu wote na haikulengwa kwa watoto chini ya miaka 13. Hatukusanyi taarifa zao kwa makusudi.

Mabadiliko na mawasiliano

Sera hii ikibadilika, tutasasisha ukurasa huu na tarehe iliyo juu. Kwa maswali, tuma barua pepe kwa info@churchycodes.co.tz au angalia ukurasa wa msaada.
