Common questions

How do I change the language?
Open Settings (the person icon at the top of Home) → Language and choose English or Kiswahili. “Phone” follows your phone’s language.

The app uses a lot of mobile data.
Open Settings → Data saver. On “Auto” the app loads smaller pictures and checks for new content less often when you are on mobile data. Download podcast episodes on Wi-Fi to listen without internet.

I’m not getting notifications.
Open Settings → Notifications and turn on the ones you want. If your phone has notifications off for BTG Ministry, turn them on in your phone’s Settings.

I forgot my password.
On the sign-in screen, tap “Forgot your password?” and we will email you a code.

How do I delete my account?
Settings → Delete my account. See deleting your account.

Maswali ya kawaida

Nabadilishaje lugha?
Fungua Mipangilio (alama ya mtu juu ya Nyumbani) → Lugha uchague Kiingereza au Kiswahili. “Simu” hufuata lugha ya simu yako.

Programu inatumia data nyingi.
Fungua Mipangilio → Kuokoa data. Kwenye “Kiotomatiki” programu hupakia picha ndogo na kuangalia maudhui mapya mara chache ukiwa kwenye data ya simu. Pakua vipindi vya sauti ukiwa kwenye Wi-Fi ili usikilize bila intaneti.

Sipati arifa.
Fungua Mipangilio → Arifa uwashe unazotaka. Ikiwa simu yako imezima arifa za BTG Ministry, ziwashe kwenye Mipangilio ya simu.

Nimesahau nenosiri.
Kwenye skrini ya kuingia, gusa “Umesahau nenosiri?” nasi tutakutumia msimbo kwa barua pepe.

Nafutaje akaunti yangu?
Mipangilio → Futa akaunti yangu. Angalia kufuta akaunti.

Soma sera yetu ya faragha.
